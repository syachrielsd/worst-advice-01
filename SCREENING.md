# Screening — 18 variants, pairwise tournament vs the judge brief

Judge criteria, in order: (1) worst advice, specific enough to follow;
(2) funny on first read; (3) tight; (4) confident AI-assistant voice;
(5) concrete situation/action. Doctrine: micro-story — advice given,
human follows it, the concrete cost lands in the last beat; odd
specifics; delete-the-ending test (line must die without its ending).

## Kills on the delete-the-ending test
- 16: not bad advice at all (whimsy, no cost) — fails criterion 1.
- 18: garbled agency (applicants? CEO's account?) — joke needs explaining.
- 9, 13: cost never lands; ending is a comment, not a consequence.
- 11, 12: familiar scam/gag shapes; ending restates the setup.
- 17: shock carries it; brief says shock alone does not count.
- 6, 14, 15: twist sits mid-line; last beat is a shrug ("writes itself").

## Semifinal
- 1 vs 3: 3 wins — the 2:47 a.m. specific and the girlfriend screenshot
  land the cost in the last beat; 1's "back you up" is sarcasm, no cost.
- 5 vs 7: 7 wins — both are tidy logic jokes, but 7 names a real scene
  (pulled over, 90 in a 35) and "a form for exactly that" is a cost.
- 4 vs 8: 4 wins — 6:10/6:30 clock pressure is a story; 8 is a flat gag.
- 2 vs 10: 2 wins — 10's cost is implied embarrassment; 2's cost is death
  delivered deadpan in three words.

## Final: 2 vs 3 vs 7 vs 4
- 4: consequence (missing your wedding) is only implied by "entrance".
- 3: strong, but the advice (text your ex) is human-bad, less AI-plausible.
- 7: very tight, very AI, but the cost is a ticket — low stakes.
- 2 WINS: criterion 1 — the advice is catastrophic and literally
  followable (drive faster); criterion 2 — the turn lands on first read;
  criterion 3 — 118 chars, three short beats, zero waste; criterion 4 —
  "Good news:" plus tidy reassurance is peak assistant voice; criterion 5
  — brakes, squeak, 40 mph. Delete-the-ending test: without "Eventually,
  so do you" the line is a flat tip; with it, the setup re-reads as a
  death sentence the AI delivered cheerfully. The cost IS the ending.

## Stop-slop pass on the winner
No adverbs doing vague work, no em dashes, no throat-clearing, active
voice throughout, every word load-bearing. "Good news:" kept — it is the
AI-voice tell the brief rewards, not filler.
